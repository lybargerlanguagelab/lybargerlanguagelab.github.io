Hadeel Elyazori

PhD Student, Information Technology, George Mason University

Hadeel is a fifth-year PhD student in Information Technology at George Mason University, specializing in natural language processing in clinical settings and correctional systems. As a recipient of the IDIA fellowship, she focuses on the implementation of motivational interviewing techniques in clinical settings, exploring methods to improve patient engagement and healthcare outcomes. She holds a master's degree in Applied Information Technology with a Machine Learning concentration from GMU, and a bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the American University of Beirut.

Research Interests

  • Clinical NLP
  • Motivational interviewing
  • Patient engagement
  • Correctional system informatics

Research Projects

  • Understanding Chronic Pain through Language

    Annotation and extraction methods for capturing patients' lived experience of chronic pain from motivational-interviewing transcripts. NIH-funded.

  • Conversational AI for Depression Management

    Causal-AI and patient-simulation methods for evaluating antidepressant-selection guidance from conversational agents. PCORI-funded.

  • NLP for Correctional & Legal Records

    Extracting information from probation and parole supervisory condition notes.
