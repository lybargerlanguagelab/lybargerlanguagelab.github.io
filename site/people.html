People

Faculty

Kevin Lybarger

Assistant Professor

Dr. Kevin Lybarger's research centers on artificial intelligence (AI) and natural language processing (NLP), particularly within healthcare applications. His interests span clinical informatics, health-oriented conversational agents, and public health. His broader research includes information integrity and correctional system informatics, using textual data to support decision-making, improve information quality, and enable innovative information exchange with societal impact. Dr. Lybarger earned his PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Washington, an MS from the University of Colorado Boulder, and a BS from Seattle University. He completed a National Library of Medicine Postdoctoral Fellowship at the University of Washington School of Medicine.

Students

  • Md. Mushfiqur Rahman

    PhD Student

    Muhammad is a fourth-year CS PhD student at GMU, specializing in NLP, computer vision, and machine learning. He has authored eight peer-reviewed papers, including Q1 journals and ACL workshop publications.   • Hadeel Elyazori

    PhD Student

    Hadeel is a fourth-year PhD student in Information Technology at George Mason University, specializing in natural language processing in clinical settings and correctional systems. As a recipient of the IDIA fellowship, she focuses on the implementation of motivational interviewing techniques in clinical settings, exploring methods to improve patient engagement and healthcare outcomes. She holds a master's degree in Applied Information Technology with a Machine Learning concentration from GMU, and a bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the American University of Beirut.

  • Mohammad Sabik Irbaz

    PhD Student

  • Saadat Hasan Khan

    PhD Student

    Saadat is a PhD student in Computer Science at George Mason University, specializing in natural language processing and its applications in healthcare. His work focuses on optimizing predictive language models and applying AI to real-world challenges in healthcare. In the summer of 2024, he interned as a Machine Learning Engineer at Brillient Corporation, where he contributed to building RAG pipeline-based medical conversational agents.   • Md Tanvir Rouf Shawon

    PhD Student

    Tanvir is a PhD student in Computer Science at George Mason University, specializing in natural language processing and healthcare-focused AI. His research explores LLM-based patient simulation and clinically reliable medical conversation systems. Before pursuing his PhD, he served as a Lecturer in the Department of CSE at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST), Bangladesh, for three years. He completed his BSc from AUST, graduating with the Dean's Award for academic excellence.
