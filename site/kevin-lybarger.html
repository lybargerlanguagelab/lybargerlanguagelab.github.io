Kevin Lybarger

Assistant Professor, Department of Information Sciences and Technology, George Mason University

Dr. Kevin Lybarger's research centers on artificial intelligence and natural language processing within healthcare applications: clinical informatics, health-oriented conversational agents, and public health. His broader research includes information integrity and correctional system informatics. He earned his PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Washington, an MS from the University of Colorado Boulder, and a BS from Seattle University, and completed a National Library of Medicine Postdoctoral Fellowship at the University of Washington School of Medicine.
